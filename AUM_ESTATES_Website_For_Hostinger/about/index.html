Our Story & Heritage

Twenty-Two Years of Unbroken Trust in Gurgaon

Founded in 2002 by Ajay Mukhija, AUM ESTATES was established with a singular conviction: that high-value real estate decisions should be guided by objective market intelligence, meticulous title due diligence, and absolute fiduciary discretion—not transactional sales pressure.

Over the past two decades, Gurgaon has transformed from an emerging suburb into one of India's preeminent financial and residential hubs. Throughout this evolution, AUM ESTATES has stood as a steadfast, trusted advisor to high-net-worth families, corporate leaders, and NRI investors navigating acquisitions across DLF Phase 1–5, Golf Course Road, Sohna Road, and Dwarka Expressway.

How We Advise Our Clients

Unlike generic real-estate aggregators that list unverified inventory, every property represented by AUM ESTATES undergoes a multi-point verification protocol before entering our public or confidential portfolio.

HRERA & Title Diligence

Rigorous verification of Haryana RERA registrations, HSVP/HUDA allotment records, encumbrances, and approved building plans.

Data-Driven Valuation

Real-time market comparables, recent registry values, and rental yields to ensure clients acquire properties at justifiable market prices.

Confidential Sourcing

Direct representation for prime builder floors, luxury condominiums, and land mandates that owners choose not to publish openly.

Structured Execution

Hands-on coordination from initial shortlist through price negotiation, statutory documentation, banking, and sub-registrar closing.
