OUR PHILOSOPHY & FOUNDING

A Fiduciary Stand in a Speculative Market

AUM ESTATES was founded in 2002 by Ajay Mukhija with a singular, resolute objective: to bring forensic clarity, rigorous due diligence, and absolute fiduciary responsibility to high-value real estate transactions in Gurgaon.

Over the past twenty-two years, Gurgaon has transformed from emergent suburbs into India's premier financial and luxury residential hub. Throughout this explosive growth, AUM ESTATES has operated not as transaction-volume brokers, but as trusted private advisors to families, corporate principals, and non-resident Indians looking to deploy capital with confidence.

“In real estate advisory, your most important work is not the deals you conclude — it is the transactions you advise your clients to walk away from when title or pricing fundamentals do not align.”

Three Pillars of Transaction Diligence

Every transaction represented by AUM ESTATES is governed by three non-negotiable principles:

1. Forensic Document & Title Verification

We conduct independent, direct verification of HSVP/HUDA records, original title deeds, non-encumbrance certificates, approved architectural sanctions, and registry chains before presenting any asset to our clients.

2. Discretion & Confidential Sourcing

Many of Gurgaon's most prestigious residences — from penthouses on Golf Course Road to sprawling builder floors in DLF Phase 1 & 2 — trade strictly off-market. We provide discreet representation that protects client confidentiality throughout the transaction lifecycle.

3. Permanent Physical Roots in Nirvana Country

Unlike virtual agencies or transient channel partners, our headquarters have remained permanently stationed at C-210 Nirvana Courtyard, Sector 50, Gurgaon since inception. Our physical presence represents accountability, accessibility, and deep local integration.
